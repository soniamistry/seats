Your seats

How it works

  • Tap an open seat to reserve it. Tap Release in your list to give one up.
  • Seats are first come, first served, and you can reserve as many as you need.
  • Rows A to E are the reservable rows. Rows nearer the stage are not reservable online, and rows behind them are general seating.
  • Alumni choose their seats on the day of the concert.
